# DESIGN SYSTEM CONTRACT: SmartMoney US v2.0 (Deep Interactive Fintech Edition)

> Modeled on US personal finance leaders (**SmartAsset**, **NerdWallet**, **Bankrate**, **Calculator.net**) and locked to `ui-ux-pro-max`, `frontend-design`, `anti-slop`, and `frontend-ui-engineering`.

## 1. Core Depth Principles (v2.0)
- **3-Second First Impression:** Clean Swiss Editorial Fintech typography (`Newsreader` + `IBM Plex Sans`), instant 1-click **Quick Scenario Presets** tailored to real US earners, live hero result cards, and crisp interactive visuals.
- **Decision-Grade Depth Across All 5 Tools:**
  1. **50-State Paycheck:** Side-by-side **State A vs. State B** take-home comparison, **2026 Federal Marginal Bracket Ladder** (10% to 37% visual bars), Marginal vs. Effective rate separation, 401(k) + HSA **Tax Shield** savings, and Smart Insight explanation.
  2. **Compound Interest & S&P 500:** Inflation-adjusted **Real Purchasing Power**, side-by-side benchmark comparison (vs. HYSA 4.5% or +$200/mo), interactive milestone bar chart, full **Year-by-Year Growth Schedule**, and **Export CSV**.
  3. **US Mortgage (PITI):** **Extra Monthly Payment** & **Biweekly Payoff** gamified reward banner (years cut + interest saved), PITI stacked bar, **Year-by-Year Amortization Schedule**, and **Export CSV**.
  4. **Auto Loan (20/4/10 Rule):** Individual 3-pillar pass/warn scorecard + **36 / 48 / 60 / 72-Month Term Comparison Table** exposing hidden long-term interest traps.
  5. **1099 Freelance Tax:** Quick deduction builder (Home Office, Equipment, Mileage), QBI 20% deduction transparency, and **2026 IRS Quarterly Due-Date Schedule** (Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Jan 15).
- **Retention & Utility:** Auto-save inputs to `localStorage`, Print / Save PDF report layout (`@media print`), Copy Summary, and Reset Defaults.
