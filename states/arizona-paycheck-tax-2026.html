How Arizona Taxes Your Paycheck in 2026

Arizona boasts the lowest flat state income tax rate in the United States in 2026 at just 2.50%—applying uniformly to all W-2 salaries, bonuses, RSUs, and 1099 earnings across Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tucson, and Mesa, with zero local city or county paycheck income taxes anywhere in the state. Arizona also matches the full federal standard deduction ($15,000 Single / $30,000 Married Filing Jointly).

Real Take-Home Pay Analysis for Arizona Earners

On a $100,000 salary in Phoenix or Scottsdale in 2026, a Single filer pays federal income tax, 7.65% FICA, and just ~$2,250 in Arizona state tax—taking home roughly $76,540/year (~$6,378/month or ~$2,944 biweekly). Married couples filing jointly at $100,000 keep roughly $82,170/year (~$6,848/month)! Combined with Arizona's ultra-low ~0.56% average property tax, relocating from California to Phoenix on a $100k–$200k salary routinely unlocks $3,000 to $14,000+ in annual after-tax savings without giving up Southwest sunshine.
