How California Taxes Your Paycheck in 2026

California has the most progressive state income tax structure in the United States in 2026, with nine statutory brackets starting at 1% and climbing to 12.3% (plus an extra 1% Mental Health Services Tax above $1,000,000, totaling 13.3%). On top of state income tax, California W-2 employees also pay California State Disability Insurance (CA SDI), which has no wage cap in 2026—meaning even high-earning Bay Area and Los Angeles salaries pay payroll SDI on every dollar earned.

Real Take-Home Pay Analysis for California Earners

On a $100,000 gross salary in California in 2026, a Single filer pays federal progressive income tax, 7.65% FICA (Social Security + Medicare), and roughly $4,800–$5,500+ in California state income tax (before pre-tax 401(k) or HSA deductions). While California does not levy local city income taxes in Los Angeles, San Francisco, or San Diego, its steep state brackets make pre-tax workplace benefits far more valuable than in 0%-tax states. Note one critical California quirk: California does NOT conform to federal HSA tax deductions—meaning HSA contributions save you federal tax, whereas Traditional 401(k) contributions cut BOTH your federal 22%–24% bracket and your California 9.3% bracket simultaneously.

4 Smart Ways to Boost Your California Take-Home Pay in 2026

  • Prioritize Traditional 401(k) contributions ($23,500+ limit) in California: every $10,000 deferred can save $3,100–$3,700 in combined Federal + CA state tax.
  • Remember California taxes HSA contributions and HSA earnings at the state level—invest HSA funds in Treasury-exempt or low-turnover index funds and track basis carefully.
  • If evaluating a relocation offer between San Francisco/LA and Austin or Seattle, compare after-tax take-home pay side by side—a $100k salary in Texas often nets $5,000+ more per year.
  • Check your W-4 and California DE-4 withholding forms after receiving RSU vests or annual bonuses to avoid April underpayment penalties.

Frequently Asked Questions: California Paycheck & Salary Taxes

How much is take-home pay on a $100,000 salary in California in 2026?

For a Single filer taking the standard deduction with no pre-tax 401(k), a $100,000 salary nets roughly $74,000–$75,000/yr (~$6,170/mo) after federal income tax, FICA, and California state income tax (slightly less after CA SDI payroll withholding).

Do Los Angeles or San Francisco charge a local city wage tax?

No. Unlike New York City or Philadelphia, California cities do not levy a local personal income tax on W-2 employees.

Does a Traditional 401(k) reduce California state income tax?

Yes. Traditional 401(k) and 403(b) contributions reduce both Federal and California taxable wages, unlike HSAs which are only tax-deductible on your federal return.
