How Georgia Taxes Your Paycheck in 2026

Georgia transitioned from its old six-bracket income tax system to a clean, flat 5.39% state income tax rate (scheduled to step down gradually toward 4.99% as state revenue triggers are met) with zero local Atlanta or county paycheck income taxes in 2026. Georgia also expanded its state standard deduction to $12,000 for Single filers and $24,000 for Married Filing Jointly households, simplifying payroll withholding across Metro Atlanta, Savannah, and Augusta.

Frequently Asked Questions: Georgia Paycheck & Salary Taxes

How much take-home pay is $100,000 in Georgia (Atlanta) in 2026?

Does the City of Atlanta charge a local paycheck income tax?

Yes. Georgia conforms to federal pre-tax rules for Traditional 401(k), 403(b), and HSA payroll contributions.
