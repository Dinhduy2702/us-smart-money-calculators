How Illinois Taxes Your Paycheck in 2026

Illinois levies a constitutionally mandated flat 4.95% state income tax on W-2 wages and 1099 income in 2026—with zero local Chicago city income tax and zero state disability payroll withholdings. Because the rate never climbs above 4.95% even at $300,000 or $1,000,000+ of income, Illinois is far friendlier to high earners than coastal states like California (13.3%), New York (10.9%+NYC), or New Jersey (10.75%).

On a $100,000 salary in Chicago or suburban Illinois in 2026, a Single filer pays federal income tax, 7.65% FICA, and roughly $4,455 in Illinois state tax—taking home around $74,335/year (~$6,195/month).
