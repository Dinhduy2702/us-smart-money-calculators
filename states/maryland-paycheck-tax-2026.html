How Maryland Taxes Your Paycheck in 2026

Maryland's headline state income tax rate (2% up to 5.75% above $250,000 Single) looks moderate on paper—until you add Maryland's mandatory County Local Income Tax! Every single Maryland county and Baltimore City levies a local 'piggyback' income tax directly on your W-2 paycheck ranging from 2.25% (Worcester) up to 3.20% in Montgomery County, Prince George's County, Howard County, and Baltimore City—pushing true combined Maryland state + county marginal rates to roughly 7.95%–8.95%.

Real Take-Home Pay Analysis for Maryland Earners

On a $100,000 salary in Bethesda (Montgomery County), Columbia (Howard County), or Baltimore in 2026, a Single filer pays federal income tax, 7.65% FICA, roughly $4,100–$4,300 in Maryland state tax, AND another ~$2,800–$3,000 in mandatory 3.2% county tax! That brings combined MD state + county take-home pay to roughly ~$71,700–$72,200/year (~$6,000/month)—roughly $2,500–$3,000/year LESS than an identical $100,000 salary just across the Potomac River in Northern Virginia (zero county income tax).

Frequently Asked Questions: Maryland Paycheck & Salary Taxes

How much take-home pay is $100,000 in Maryland in 2026?

After federal tax, FICA, and Maryland state tax, net pay is ~$74,644/yr before county tax—and roughly ~$71,800/yr (~$5,983/mo) once Montgomery, Howard, Prince George's, or Baltimore City's 3.20% county tax is included.

Do all Maryland counties charge a local paycheck income tax?

Yes. Maryland is unique in that ALL 23 counties plus Baltimore City levy a mandatory local income tax (2.25% to 3.20%) withheld automatically on W-2 paychecks.

Why do Northern Virginia paychecks net more than Maryland paychecks?

Virginia caps state tax at 5.75% with 0% county income tax, whereas Maryland adds a 2.25%–3.20% county income tax on top of its 4.75%–5.75% state rate.
