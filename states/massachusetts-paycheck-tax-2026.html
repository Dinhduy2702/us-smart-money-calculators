How Massachusetts Taxes Your Paycheck in 2026

Massachusetts taxes W-2 wages, salaries, and bonuses at a flat 5.0% state income tax rate in 2026, with zero local Boston city income tax. Only ultra-high earners whose annual Massachusetts taxable income exceeds the ~$1 million inflation-adjusted threshold pay an additional 4.0% 'Fair Share' Millionaire Surtax on the excess. Massachusetts W-2 employees also contribute a modest payroll share toward MA Paid Family and Medical Leave (PFML, ~0.46% up to the Social Security wage cap).

Real Take-Home Pay Analysis for Massachusetts Earners

On a $100,000 salary in Boston, Cambridge, or Worcester in 2026, a Single filer pays federal income tax, 7.65% FICA, and approximately $4,500 in Massachusetts state income tax—leaving roughly $74,290/year (~$6,190/month) before MA PFML (~$460/yr). For Married couples filing jointly at $100,000, take-home pay is roughly $79,920/year (~$6,660/month). Compared to New York City, a Boston biotech or software engineer earning $150,000+ often saves $4,000 to $7,000+ per year in combined state and local paycheck taxes.

4 Smart Ways to Boost Your Massachusetts Take-Home Pay in 2026

  • Max out Traditional 401(k), HSA, and commuter transit deductions to shave both federal (22%–35%) and Massachusetts 5.0% taxes off every paycheck.
  • Claim the Massachusetts Renters Deduction on your state return (up to 50% of rent paid, capped at $4,000/yr)—an easy $200 cash savings for Greater Boston renters.
  • Remember Massachusetts taxes short-term capital gains at 8.5% vs. 5.0% for long-term gains, so coordinate brokerage sales alongside your paycheck plan.
  • If your combined salary + RSU vesting approaches the ~$1M Fair Share surtax threshold, maximize pre-tax deferrals and time equity sales across tax years.

Frequently Asked Questions: Massachusetts Paycheck & Salary Taxes

How much take-home pay is $100,000 in Massachusetts (Boston) in 2026?

A Single filer earning $100,000 in Massachusetts nets roughly $74,290/year (~$6,191/month) after federal income tax, FICA, and the flat 5.0% state tax (minus ~$460/yr for MA PFML).

Does Boston have a local city wage tax?

No. Boston and all other Massachusetts municipalities charge 0% local income tax on paychecks.

Does the 4% Massachusetts Millionaire Surtax affect a $100k–$300k salary?

No. The extra 4% surtax only applies to taxable income ABOVE the ~$1 million inflation-adjusted threshold.
