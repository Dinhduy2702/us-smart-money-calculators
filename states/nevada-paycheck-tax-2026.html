How Nevada Taxes Your Paycheck in 2026

Nevada levies 0% state personal income tax in 2026 on W-2 wages, bonuses, tech RSUs, crypto/stock gains, and 1099 freelance income—protected by Nevada's constitution and funded largely by tourism, gaming, and sales taxes. With zero local city wage taxes in Las Vegas, Henderson, and Reno—and a low ~0.50% effective property tax rate—Nevada has become the #1 tax-migration destination for remote California tech workers and founders.
