How New York Taxes Your Paycheck in 2026

New York imposes progressive state income tax rates ranging from 4% up to 10.9% in 2026—and if you live inside the five boroughs of New York City (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx, Staten Island), you pay an additional NYC local resident income tax of 3.078% to 3.876% on every paycheck. New York W-2 employees also contribute modest payroll amounts to NY Paid Family Leave (PFL) and NY State Disability (SDI).

Real Take-Home Pay Analysis for New York Earners

A Single filer earning $100,000 in New York State pays federal income tax, 7.65% FICA, and roughly $5,000 in New York State income tax—leaving around $73,800/year (~$6,150/month) before NYC local tax. If you live inside New York City, that extra ~3.5%–3.876% city resident tax trims another ~$3,200–$3,600 off annual take-home pay, bringing net pay closer to ~$70,300/year. Importantly, New York's 'convenience of the employer' rule means remote workers assigned to a New York office can still owe NY State tax even if working from another state, though non-residents of NYC do NOT pay the 3.876% NYC resident tax.

4 Smart Ways to Boost Your New York Take-Home Pay in 2026

  • Max out Traditional 401(k), HSA, and Pre-Tax Commuter Transit Benefits (MTA/LIRR/Metro-North)—in NYC, pre-tax deductions shield you from Federal, NY State, AND NYC local taxes all at once.
  • Compare living in Hoboken/Jersey City or Westchester vs. Manhattan: moving outside the five boroughs eliminates the 3.078%–3.876% NYC local income tax immediately.
  • Remember that bonus checks in New York are often withheld at flat supplemental rates (22% federal + 11.7% NY State + 4.25% NYC), which may result in a refund at tax time.
  • Pair your paycheck planning with New York's 529 college savings deduction (up to $5,000 Single / $10,000 Married deductible on NY State returns).

Frequently Asked Questions: New York Paycheck & Salary Taxes

How much is a $100,000 salary after taxes in New York in 2026?

Outside NYC, a Single filer earning $100,000 nets roughly $73,770/year (~$6,148/month). Inside New York City, adding the ~3.5%–3.876% NYC local tax brings take-home closer to ~$70,300/year.

Do commuters from New Jersey or Connecticut pay NYC city income tax?

No. Only residents of the five NYC boroughs pay NYC local income tax. Commuters from NJ or CT pay NY State non-resident tax and receive a credit on their home-state return.

Does New York tax HSA contributions like California does?

No. New York conforms to federal HSA rules, so HSA contributions are tax-deductible for both Federal and New York State/NYC income taxes.
