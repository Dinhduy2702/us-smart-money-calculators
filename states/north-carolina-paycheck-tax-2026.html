How North Carolina Taxes Your Paycheck in 2026

North Carolina levies a low flat 4.25% personal income tax rate (stepping down toward 3.99% under enacted multi-year tax cuts) with zero local city or county paycheck taxes in Charlotte, Raleigh-Durham (Research Triangle), and Asheville. North Carolina pairs that flat rate with a solid state standard deduction ($12,750 Single / $25,500 Married Filing Jointly), making it one of the most paycheck-friendly taxed states on the East Coast.

Real Take-Home Pay Analysis for North Carolina Earners

On a $100,000 salary in Charlotte or Raleigh in 2026, a Single filer pays federal income tax, 7.65% FICA, and roughly $3,825 in North Carolina state tax—netting approximately $74,965/year (~$6,247/month or ~$2,883 biweekly). For Married Filing Jointly households earning $100,000, annual take-home climbs to roughly $80,595/year (~$6,716/month). Compared to New York City or California, a tech or banking professional relocating to Charlotte or Raleigh often saves $4,500 to $10,000+ per year in state/local paycheck taxes alone.

4 Smart Ways to Boost Your North Carolina Take-Home Pay in 2026

  • Maximize Traditional 401(k) and HSA payroll deferrals—North Carolina starts from Federal AGI, so pre-tax workplace deductions cut both federal and NC 4.25% taxes automatically.
  • If relocating from NY/NJ/CA to Charlotte or Raleigh, update your NC-4 withholding certificate immediately so payroll stops withholding your old high-tax state.
  • Pair North Carolina's low 4.25% income tax with its low ~0.73% average property tax when evaluating Rent vs. Buy decisions on HomeMath US.
  • Note that North Carolina does not offer a state income tax deduction for 529 contributions, so prioritize 401(k)/HSA/Roth IRA buckets first.

Frequently Asked Questions: North Carolina Paycheck & Salary Taxes

How much is $100,000 after taxes in North Carolina in 2026?

A Single filer earning $100,000 in North Carolina takes home approximately $74,965/year (~$6,247/month) after federal tax, FICA, and NC's 4.25% flat state tax.

Do Charlotte or Raleigh charge a local city wage tax?

No. North Carolina prohibits local municipalities from charging personal income taxes on paychecks.

Is North Carolina's income tax rate flat for all salaries?

Yes. Every taxable dollar—from $25,000 to $1,000,000+—is taxed at the exact same flat North Carolina rate (4.25% stepping toward 3.99%).
