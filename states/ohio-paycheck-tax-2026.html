How Ohio Taxes Your Paycheck in 2026

Ohio drastically compressed its state income tax brackets to one of the lowest ceilings in the Midwest in 2026: 0% on the first $26,050 of taxable income, 2.75% up to $100,000, and a top rate of just 3.5% above $100,000. However, just like Pennsylvania, Ohio cities and villages levy separate municipal income taxes (collected via RITA, CCA, or city tax offices) typically ranging from 1.5% to 2.5% in Columbus (2.5%), Cleveland (2.5%), and Cincinnati (1.8%).

Real Take-Home Pay Analysis for Ohio Earners

On a $100,000 salary in Columbus, Cincinnati, or Cleveland in 2026, a Single filer pays federal income tax, 7.65% FICA, and roughly $2,300–$2,400 in Ohio state income tax—leaving approximately $76,470/year (~$6,373/month) before municipal city tax. Factoring in a typical 2.0%–2.5% city wage tax (~$2,000–$2,500/yr) brings true urban take-home to roughly ~$74,000/year—still giving Ohio workers far higher purchasing power than coastal metros due to affordable Midwest housing.

4 Smart Ways to Boost Your Ohio Take-Home Pay in 2026

  • Check whether your home suburb gives a 100% municipal tax credit for city taxes withheld where you work—living in a non-credit suburb can trigger an extra 0.5%–1.0% RITA bill in April!
  • Max out Traditional 401(k) and HSA deductions: they cleanly reduce your Federal and Ohio state income taxes (note: most Ohio cities still tax 401(k) deferrals locally, similar to PA).
  • If you run a side business or 1099 LLC in Ohio, claim Ohio's famous Business Income Deduction (BID)—the first $250,000 of qualified Ohio business income is 100% EXEMPT from Ohio state income tax!
  • Verify your Ohio IT-4 withholding form and local municipality code on your first paystub so you never get hit with surprise RITA penalties.

Frequently Asked Questions: Ohio Paycheck & Salary Taxes

How much take-home pay is $100,000 in Ohio in 2026?

After federal tax, FICA, and Ohio state tax, a Single filer nets roughly $76,470/year (~$6,373/month), or ~$74,000–$74,500/year after a typical 2%–2.5% Columbus/Cleveland/Cincinnati municipal wage tax.

What is Ohio's $250,000 Business Income Deduction?

Ohio exempts the first $250,000 of net 1099 / LLC / S-Corp pass-through business income ($125,000 Married Filing Separately) from Ohio state income tax, taxing any excess at a flat 3%.

Do remote workers in Ohio pay city tax where they live or where their office is?

Under Ohio law, municipal income tax is generally owed where you physically perform the work (your home city if working 100% remotely).
