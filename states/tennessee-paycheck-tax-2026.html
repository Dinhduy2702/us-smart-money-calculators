How Tennessee Taxes Your Paycheck in 2026

Tennessee imposes 0% state personal income tax on W-2 salaries, bonuses, RSUs, 1099 freelance profits, dividends, and retirement distributions in 2026 (having fully repealed the old Hall Income Tax on investment income). With zero local city wage taxes in Nashville, Franklin, Knoxville, Chattanooga, and Memphis—plus one of the lowest average property tax rates in America (~0.56%)—Tennessee is one of the highest take-home-pay states in the entire country.

Real Take-Home Pay Analysis for Tennessee Earners

On a $100,000 salary in Nashville or Knoxville in 2026, a Single filer pays ONLY federal progressive income tax and 7.65% FICA—netting approximately $78,790/year (~$6,566/month or ~$3,030 biweekly). Married Filing Jointly households earning $100,000 keep roughly $84,420/year (~$7,035/month). Unlike Texas (1.63% property tax) or Florida (high hurricane insurance), Tennessee delivers 0% paycheck tax WITHOUT punitive property taxes, funding state services largely through a ~9.55% combined state + local sales tax.
