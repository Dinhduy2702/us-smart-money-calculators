How Texas Taxes Your Paycheck in 2026

Texas levies 0% state personal income tax on W-2 wages, bonuses, RSUs, and 1099 freelance earnings in 2026. Protected by a constitutional prohibition against individual income taxes, employees in Austin, Dallas-Fort Worth, Houston, and San Antonio only see two mandatory tax lines on their paystubs: federal progressive income tax and 7.65% federal FICA (6.2% Social Security up to the $176,100 wage cap plus 1.45% Medicare).

Frequently Asked Questions: Texas Paycheck & Salary Taxes

How much take-home pay is $100,000 a year in Texas in 2026?

A Single filer earning $100,000 in Texas takes home approximately $78,790 per year ($6,566/month or $3,030 every two weeks) after federal income tax and FICA.

Are there any city income taxes in Austin, Dallas, or Houston?

No. Texas has zero state income tax and zero local city or county income taxes on paychecks.

Do Texas residents still pay FICA (Social Security and Medicare)?

Yes. Federal FICA taxes (6.2% Social Security up to $176,100 and 1.45% Medicare) apply nationwide in all 50 states, including Texas.
