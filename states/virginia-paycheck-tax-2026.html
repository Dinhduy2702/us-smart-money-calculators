How Virginia Taxes Your Paycheck in 2026

Virginia taxes personal income across four brackets—2%, 3%, 5%, and a top rate of 5.75% on taxable income above $17,000 in 2026. While the 5.75% top rate starts early, Virginia charges zero local city or county income tax (unlike neighboring Maryland's 2.25%–3.2% county piggyback taxes or Washington, D.C.'s 10.75% top rate) and zero state disability payroll withholdings, making Northern Virginia (Arlington, Fairfax, Loudoun) the most tax-efficient part of the D.C. metro area.

Real Take-Home Pay Analysis for Virginia Earners

On a $100,000 salary in Northern Virginia, Richmond, or Virginia Beach in 2026, a Single filer pays federal income tax, 7.65% FICA, and roughly $4,250–$4,800 in Virginia state income tax—taking home approximately $74,500/year (~$6,210/month). Married couples filing jointly at $100,000 keep roughly $80,100/year (~$6,675/month). Virginia also offers a generous state standard deduction ($8,500 Single / $17,000 Married) and conforms cleanly to federal Traditional 401(k) and HSA pre-tax deductions.

4 Smart Ways to Boost Your Virginia Take-Home Pay in 2026

  • If working in Washington, D.C., remember the D.C.–Virginia reciprocity agreement: D.C. workers living in Virginia pay ONLY Virginia's 5.75% rate, NOT D.C.'s 8.5%–10.75% brackets!
  • Max out Traditional 401(k) and HSA contributions to cut both federal (22%–24%) and Virginia 5.75% marginal taxes on every paycheck.
  • Verify your employer is withholding Virginia tax (not D.C. or Maryland tax) by submitting Form D-4A if you commute into Washington, D.C.
  • Take advantage of Virginia's 529 deduction (up to $4,000 per account per year) to further reduce your Virginia state tax bill.

Frequently Asked Questions: Virginia Paycheck & Salary Taxes

How much is $100,000 after taxes in Virginia in 2026?

A Single filer earning $100,000 in Virginia takes home roughly $74,550/year (~$6,212/month) after federal income tax, FICA, and Virginia state tax.

Do Fairfax or Arlington counties charge a local paycheck income tax?

No. Unlike Maryland counties, Virginia counties and cities do not charge local personal income taxes on W-2 paychecks.

If I live in Virginia and commute to Washington, D.C., which state's tax do I pay?

You pay ONLY Virginia state income tax thanks to the reciprocal tax agreement between Virginia and D.C.
